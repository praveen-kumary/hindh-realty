Our Story

Building trust,
one plot at a time.

For over a decade, Hindh Group has been a quiet, deliberate force in shaping North Bangalore's most coveted neighbourhoods — driven by a single conviction: that land is the most enduring legacy a family can build.

The Beginning

One man. One vision.

Hindh Group India Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2012 by Syed Parveez Ahmed — a visionary leader with an unshakeable conviction: every plot he sold would be one he himself would proudly buy for his family.

That single principle became the company's compass. Every layout we design, every approval we secure, every road we lay — it begins with one question: "Would I want my family to live here?"

Twelve years and six landmark projects later, that compass still points true. 2,800+ families across North Bangalore call a Hindh address home — and that, more than any marketing brochure, is our proudest credential.

Founder & Chairman

Syed Parveez Ahmed

Mr. Syed Parveez Ahmed specialises in conceptualising and executing innovative structures for both residential and commercial projects. With a distinguished career spanning over 15 years, he has worked on some of the most unique and high-profile developments in the North Bangalore real estate industry — earning a reputation for vision, precision, and unwavering quality standards.

Syed Parveez Ahmed's faith in family values drives Hindh Group's core philosophy — striving to build sustainable, long-lasting relationships with every buyer through clear communication, transparency, and mutual respect. Under his leadership, Hindh Group has grown from a single-project venture into one of the region's most trusted names, with 2,800+ proud families calling a Hindh address home.

He personally oversees every land parcel — evaluating legal clarity, infrastructure potential, and long-term appreciation before a single plot is approved for sale. His hands-on approach to quality and compliance is the foundation upon which six landmark projects have been successfully delivered.
