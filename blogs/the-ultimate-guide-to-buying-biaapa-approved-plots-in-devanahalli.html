Frequently Asked Questions: ROI & Investment Strategy

Are plots a better investment than apartments in Bangalore?

Yes. While apartments depreciate over time due to building aging and offer limited capital appreciation, land is a finite resource that consistently appreciates. A premium plot in a BIAAPA approved gated community in North Bangalore historically yields 3x higher returns over a 10-year period compared to luxury apartments, while involving zero maintenance costs.

What are the advantages of buying in a gated community versus independent land?

Independent land carries risks of encroachment, requires personal effort for legal verification, and lacks infrastructure. A Hindh Group premium plotted development offers 24/7 security, master-planned infrastructure (wide roads, underground electricity, water connections), luxurious amenities, and immediate bank-loan eligibility, ensuring your asset is both secure and highly liquid.

When is the right time to sell a plot for maximum profit?

Real estate wealth is built through patience. We advise investors to hold plots for a minimum of 5 to 7 years to ride the full wave of infrastructure development (like the Metro completion and ITIR operationalization). By aligning your exit strategy with major government project deliveries, you maximize your capital gains exponentially.
